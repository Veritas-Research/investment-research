## How I work

- **Dated and versioned.** Every piece carries its publish date and version, and I write follow-ups instead of quietly editing the original.
- **Numbers first.** Every claim gets a table, a multiple, or a scenario, and every number gets a source.
- **Scenario weighted.** I give probability bands, not single predictions, and size positions to match.
- **Built to be wrong in public.** Each piece says what would break the thesis, and the follow-ups say whether it broke.
